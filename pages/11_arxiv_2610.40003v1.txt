Aoqian Zhang1,*, Chunzheng Wang1,*, Haoran Chen1,*, Annan Tang2,
Cheng Xiang3, Fan Shi1, and Shuzhi Sam Ge1
Abstract—Humanoidshavebecomeincreasinglyagileandca-
pableinreal-worldenvironments.Tooperatesafelyindynamic
scenarios, they must rapidly evade fast-moving obstacles while
maintainingstabilityandphysicallyfeasiblewhole-bodymotion.
Learning such behaviors remains challenging due to the high-
dimensional dynamics of humanoid systems and the need to
jointlybalancereactiveobstacleavoidance,motionnaturalness,
and control robustness. In this work, we present Humanoid
Dodgeball, a reinforcement learning (RL) framework for agile
dynamicobstacleavoidanceinhumanoids.Ourmethodenables
highly dynamic whole-body evasive maneuvers against incom-
ing projectiles through threat-aware reward shaping, while
multiple direction-specific adversarial motion priors regularize
thelearnedbehaviorstowardstableanddiversehuman-likemo-
tions. Extensive simulation and hardware experiments demon-
strate that the proposed framework substantially improves
dynamic obstacle avoidance performance across diverse obsta-
cle speeds and launch distances, while preserving robust and
physically plausible humanoid motions. Supplementary videos
are available at https://humanoid-dodge.github.io/.
